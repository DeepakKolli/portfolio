Products I've Shipped

01
FinTechLoan Aggregator

FinTech Startup

Loan Aggregator App

🔴 The Problem

People who needed a loan had to visit each bank one by one — either physically or on their individual websites. Time-consuming, confusing, and exhausting just to compare rates and eligibility across multiple banks.

💡 The Solution

A loan aggregator app that brought all major banks into one place. Users could see loan offers, interest rates, and eligibility side by side — then apply directly to the bank of their choice, all from a single screen.

🛠️ What I Did as PM

I owned this product from day zero to Play Store launch. Wrote the full PRD, ran sessions to understand user pain points, collaborated with designers in Figma, managed engineering via Jira, and documented everything in Confluence. After launch, I continued monitoring user feedback and iterated with the team.

✦ Result: Significant growth in app users post-launch. Users could compare and apply for loans in minutes instead of days — saving time and reducing confusion at every step.

02
FinTechGamification

FinTech Startup

Incentive-Based Rewards App

🔴 The Problem

Financial tasks like applying for a loan have poor completion rates because users don't see an immediate reward. How do you motivate users to take meaningful financial actions in a way that feels fun, not like a chore?

💡 The Solution

An incentive-based platform where users earn rewards by completing tasks — playing games, signing up for services, or applying for financial products through partner banks. Complete the task, earn a generous incentive. Simple, motivating, and engaging.

🛠️ What I Did as PM

End-to-end product ownership — defined the task flow and reward logic, shaped the user journey, and worked hands-on with design and engineering to ship the product. Ensured the gamification loop felt natural and not forced, which was key to user retention.

✦ Result: Strong user engagement post-launch with high task completion rates. Users returned repeatedly to complete new tasks and claim rewards — proving the incentive model worked.

03
FinTechReferral & Rewards

FinTech Startup

Share & Earn Incentive App

🔴 The Problem

Growing a user base organically is expensive and slow. Most apps struggle to turn existing users into active promoters. The challenge was to build a product that rewards users for sharing and referring — making growth feel natural, not forced.

💡 The Solution

A share-and-earn incentive platform where users earn rewards every time they refer friends or share the app with their network. The more they share, the more they earn — creating a viral loop that drives organic user growth at low acquisition cost.

🛠️ What I Did as PM

Defined the referral flow and reward structure, wrote the PRD, and collaborated with design and engineering to build and launch the product. Monitored post-launch sharing patterns and iterated on the reward logic to improve referral conversion rates.

✦ Result: Increased organic user acquisition through the referral loop. Users actively shared the app to earn incentives, reducing the cost of new user acquisition significantly.

04
FinTechPayrollB2B

FinTech Startup

Prepaid Payroll Management App

🔴 The Problem

Many businesses — especially small and mid-sized companies — struggle with timely, flexible salary disbursement. Employees often wait until month-end for their pay, causing financial stress. Existing payroll tools were either too complex or not built for prepaid, on-demand salary needs.

💡 The Solution

A prepaid payroll app that allows businesses to disburse salaries early or on-demand directly to employee accounts — giving employees access to their earned wages before the traditional payday. Simple for employers to manage, and empowering for employees.

🛠️ What I Did as PM

Owned the product end-to-end — from understanding employer and employee pain points, writing the PRD, coordinating with design and development teams, to overseeing the launch. Ensured the payroll disbursement flow was simple, secure, and reliable for both business owners and employees.

✦ Result: Successfully launched a prepaid payroll solution that improved salary flexibility for employees and reduced administrative burden for businesses — addressing a real gap in the market.
